Privacy Policy

The Hidden Half — publishing application. Last updated 20 September 2026.

This policy covers the private application hidden-half-publisher, which publishes videos to the Instagram and Facebook accounts belonging to The Hidden Half. The application is operated by the owner of those accounts and is not offered to anyone else.

What the application does

It uploads videos that the operator has produced to the operator's own Instagram professional account, together with a caption. That is its only function. It has no interface for other people, no sign-up, and no way for a third party to use it.

What data is collected

None from any user. The application does not collect, request, receive, or store personal data belonging to visitors, followers, or any other person.

The only credentials it holds are the operator's own:

  • An access token for the operator's own Facebook Page and the Instagram professional account connected to it.
  • The numeric identifiers of that Page and that Instagram account.

These are stored on the operator's own machine, readable only by the operator's account, and are used solely to publish the operator's own videos.

What data is shared

Nothing is shared with anyone. No data is sold, rented, transferred, or disclosed to third parties. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Retention and deletion

Because no user data is collected, there is no user data to retain or delete. The operator's own access token is deleted by removing the local token file, and access can be revoked at any time from the Facebook account's Settings → Apps and Websites page, which immediately and permanently invalidates it.

If you believe this application holds data about you, write to the address below and it will be deleted; in practice there will be none to find.

Children

The application is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
